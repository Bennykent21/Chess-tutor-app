# Changelog

Each release's notes on GitHub come from its section here. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-03

The first public release.

### Play

- 12 bots with their own style and voice, rated 250 to 2500; between 1100 and 1900 they move like people of that rating (Maia).
- Build your own bot, Quick match, play on from any position, Opening Battle, Explore with the engine, and Watch two bots.
- Clocks, takebacks, hints and an evaluation bar.

### Learn

- 38 courses and 73 lessons: the basics, openings, tactics, the middlegame, endgames and common blunders.
- An opening trainer with about 3,900 named openings.

### Puzzles

- 13,485 puzzles from the Lichess database with a puzzle rating.
- The daily puzzle, a level path, Rush, puzzles by theme, Custom run, and your own mistakes coming back on a spaced schedule.

### Your games

- Link Chess.com or Lichess to bring in your rapid, blitz and bullet games.
- Stockfish 19 reviews each game on the phone: accuracy, move grades, key moments and better moves.
- Stats for each time control with a rating graph, and a Training page for puzzles.

### Make it yours

- Four colour themes, each in light and dark; board colours, four piece sets, sounds, vibration and Reduce motion.

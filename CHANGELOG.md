# Changelog

Each release's notes on GitHub come from its section here. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org).

## [1.2.0] - 2026-11-19

The biggest update so far: every tab was redesigned, and openings are now built from millions of real club games.

### Openings

- Twenty-nine openings to learn and drill: your White openings, and Black's answers to 1.e4, 1.d4 and the rest, filtered by one tap. Every move comes from what players at your level really play, checked by Stockfish.
- Eight openings in the new course look, each with an ideas course and its variations: London, Queen's Gambit, Ruy Lopez, English, Scandinavian, French, Sicilian and King's Indian. Every move and position in them was checked.
- Every line is taught even without words: watch it, then play it back.
- Punish mistakes: the slips players at your level really make in each opening, with how often they make them. Find the move that punishes them.
- Tactics from each opening: 60 real puzzles from games in that opening, solved by your side, from beginner to 2000.
- One page per opening, from Learn and from Train, with its video at the top.

### Learn

- Rebuilt: topics by level, progress rings, an opening page with its variations, and a talking coach that explains each move.
- Videos inside the app: each lesson can open on its own video, a course's video sits where its art was, with full screen and 10-second steps.
- Moves in plain words, and a short course on reading chess notation.
- The top card continues your last lesson.

### Train

- Today: one session that mixes what is due with mistakes to punish, then tactics from your openings.
- Drills that prove you know a line: a miss comes back later at random, two right answers after a miss, green and red dots, and a clean pass to finish.
- Drill any variation you have learnt, and a mixed Challenge per opening that grows with each cup.
- From your games, rebuilt: your numbers, your openings with their results and where you leave them, endings you let slip and mistakes you repeat, each with a way to train it.
- Your openings on the front page; drills go quicker and without the talking coach.

### Home and Play

- A new Play list, custom games, one clock picker and a new game screen.
- Premoves, several in a row.
- New games are reviewed in the background (only the newest, and you can turn it off in Settings).

### Puzzles

- Rebuilt: a level strip, one tile style, a richer goal page and custom runs.

### Account and Stats

- A cleaner Account page with your piece, your ratings and your games.
- Game pages like every other board screen, with metal result icons and a centred move strip.
- Stats: the period you pick narrows every number, a richer Insights section (results by rating and time of day, accuracy by phase, where your losses turn), and every number that stands for games opens them.

### Everywhere

- One day streak for the whole app: puzzles, games played here, lessons and drills all count.
- Explore grades a move on a deeper search and calls out a bigger win let go; stale engine lines no longer spoil its grades.
- New piece sets (Celtic, Spatial, Ink, Terracotta) chosen from a grid, more board colours, and sound packs (Studio, Piano, Retro, Sci-fi) with a preview.
- An icons-only bottom bar, outlined until chosen; metal icons across the app; no empty coach bubbles.

## [1.1.2] - 2026-11-17

### Everywhere

- No more panels sliding up from the bottom. A game's result, a cup won, a badge and short choices open as a card in the middle of the screen; filtering your games and choosing a puzzle goal get a page of their own.

### Updates

- A new version now shows on Home: an arrow beside your streak while it waits, filling up as it downloads.
- What's new: everything that changed since your version, with the update button.
- The app looks for a new version each time you open it.

## [1.1.1] - 2026-11-17

### Game review

- Accuracy closer to chess.com: tuned on six games reviewed on both, it now lands within about four points of chess.com's number. Book moves no longer count, and the accuracy for each game phase follows the same rule.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-16

### Train

- A new Train tab in the middle: opening drills that flow without Next, Win this endgame and Convert this middlegame against Stockfish, and openings against Stockfish.
- From your games: the places you leave your opening the same way, endings you let slip and mistakes you repeat, as numbers from your own games.
- Your own opening lines: build them on the board, drill them and play them out against Stockfish.

### Learn

- The Italian Game and the Caro-Kann from real games, a course per variation, and lessons that show each line before asking for it.

### Play

- Play a friend on one phone, or on two phones over the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. A game in progress is kept when the app closes.
- Chess960 against a friend or a bot.

### Game review

- Grades and accuracy much closer to chess.com: one lost piece no longer sinks a whole game, mates and lost material count in decided games, and no more false Brilliants.
- Deeper and faster: a first review within moments, then each move is checked again more deeply. The opening comes from master games and Lichess's cloud when you have internet.

### Updates

- The app tells you when a new version is out and installs it for you (Android asks you to confirm).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-03

The first public release.

### Play

- 12 bots with their own style and voice, rated 250 to 2500; between 1100 and 1900 they move like people of that rating (Maia).
- Build your own bot, Quick match, play on from any position, Opening Battle, Explore with the engine, and Watch two bots.
- Clocks, takebacks, hints and an evaluation bar.

### Learn

- 38 courses and 73 lessons: the basics, openings, tactics, the middlegame, endgames and common blunders.
- An opening trainer with about 3,900 named openings.

### Puzzles

- 13,485 puzzles from the Lichess database with a puzzle rating.
- The daily puzzle, a level path, Rush, puzzles by theme, Custom run, and your own mistakes coming back on a spaced schedule.

### Your games

- Link Chess.com or Lichess to bring in your rapid, blitz and bullet games.
- Stockfish 19 reviews each game on the phone: accuracy, move grades, key moments and better moves.
- Stats for each time control with a rating graph, and a Training page for puzzles.

### Make it yours

- Four colour themes, each in light and dark; board colours, four piece sets, sounds, vibration and Reduce motion.

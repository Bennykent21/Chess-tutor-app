# Changelog

Each release's notes on GitHub come from its section here. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org).

## [1.1.1] - 2026-11-17

### Game review

- Accuracy closer to chess.com: tuned on six games reviewed on both, it now lands within about four points of chess.com's number. Book moves no longer count, and the accuracy for each game phase follows the same rule.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-16

### Train

- A new Train tab in the middle: opening drills that flow without Next, Win this endgame and Convert this middlegame against Stockfish, and openings against Stockfish.
- From your games: the places you leave your opening the same way, endings you let slip and mistakes you repeat, as numbers from your own games.
- Your own opening lines: build them on the board, drill them and play them out against Stockfish.

### Learn

- The Italian Game and the Caro-Kann from real games, a course per variation, and lessons that show each line before asking for it.

### Play

- Play a friend on one phone, or on two phones over the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. A game in progress is kept when the app closes.
- Chess960 against a friend or a bot.

### Game review

- Grades and accuracy much closer to chess.com: one lost piece no longer sinks a whole game, mates and lost material count in decided games, and no more false Brilliants.
- Deeper and faster: a first review within moments, then each move is checked again more deeply. The opening comes from master games and Lichess's cloud when you have internet.

### Updates

- The app tells you when a new version is out and installs it for you (Android asks you to confirm).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-03

The first public release.

### Play

- 12 bots with their own style and voice, rated 250 to 2500; between 1100 and 1900 they move like people of that rating (Maia).
- Build your own bot, Quick match, play on from any position, Opening Battle, Explore with the engine, and Watch two bots.
- Clocks, takebacks, hints and an evaluation bar.

### Learn

- 38 courses and 73 lessons: the basics, openings, tactics, the middlegame, endgames and common blunders.
- An opening trainer with about 3,900 named openings.

### Puzzles

- 13,485 puzzles from the Lichess database with a puzzle rating.
- The daily puzzle, a level path, Rush, puzzles by theme, Custom run, and your own mistakes coming back on a spaced schedule.

### Your games

- Link Chess.com or Lichess to bring in your rapid, blitz and bullet games.
- Stockfish 19 reviews each game on the phone: accuracy, move grades, key moments and better moves.
- Stats for each time control with a rating graph, and a Training page for puzzles.

### Make it yours

- Four colour themes, each in light and dark; board colours, four piece sets, sounds, vibration and Reduce motion.
